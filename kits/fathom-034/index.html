The Fathom starter kit

Fathom

Setup steps, in order

  1. Sign up at fathom.ai.
  2. Connect your Google or Microsoft calendar so Fathom can join your meetings.
  3. Personalize your account.
  4. Download the desktop app and log in.
  5. Check your settings, then run a test call.

Questions to ask before you start

  • Does everyone on the call agree to be recorded?
  • Which calendar will Fathom read, and is it the right one?
  • Who reviews the summary before it is shared?
  • Which tools should the notes sync to, and who approves that?
  • Does your company allow notetakers on client calls?

Prompts to try on day one

  • Run Fathom on your next team meeting and read the summary.
  • What action items came out of today's call?
  • List the decisions we made in the team meeting and who owns each one.
  • Find where the client mentioned the budget in last week's call.
  • Draft a short follow-up email from this call summary.
