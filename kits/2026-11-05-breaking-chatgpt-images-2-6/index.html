The ChatGPT Images 2.6 starter kit

OpenAI

Setup steps, in order

  1. Ask ChatGPT to create an image in a conversation, or select More and then Images.
  2. For a GIF, describe a loop or upload a still image and ask ChatGPT to animate it.
  3. Describe edits in the conversation, then open the GIF to adjust playback speed, save it or share it.

Questions to ask before you start

  • Does company policy allow uploading work material to ChatGPT?
  • Do we have permission to use anyone's likeness in a saved reference photo?
  • Who reviews the GIF and any text in it before it goes out?

Prompts to try on day one

  • Make a looping GIF of a coffee cup with steam rising.
  • Animate this product photo into a short loop and keep the background still.
  • Slow this GIF down a little and fix the logo in one frame.
