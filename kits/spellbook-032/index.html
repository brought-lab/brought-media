The Spellbook starter kit

Spellbook

Setup steps, in order

  1. Get a license key from your Spellbook sales rep or your organization's admin.
  2. Create one Spellbook account with your work email and a verification code.
  3. Sign in to Associate and activate the license there.
  4. In Word, open the Add-ins store, search "Spellbook" and click Add.
  5. Open Spellbook in Word and sign in with the same account.

Questions to ask before you start

  • Does my team have a license, and who holds the key?
  • Does IT manage our Microsoft account, and does an admin need to deploy the add-in?
  • Have we reviewed the data-processing agreement, subprocessors and retention settings?
  • Who approves each redline before the contract goes out?
  • Is this a contract task? Spellbook is for contracts only.

Prompts to try on day one

  • Flag risks and missing terms in this agreement.
  • Review this contract against our playbook and list the gaps.
  • Draft a redline for the indemnity clause and explain the change.
  • Compare this limitation of liability clause with a benchmark.
