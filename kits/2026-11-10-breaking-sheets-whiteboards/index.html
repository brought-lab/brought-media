Anthropic

Setup steps, in order

  1. Use a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team or Enterprise) on web or desktop.
  2. On Enterprise, ask an owner to turn Sheets and Whiteboards on first.
  3. Ask in chat for something concrete like a campaign tracker, or start from a Sheets or Whiteboards template.

Questions to ask before you start

  • Is it turned on for our plan, and has an owner approved it on Enterprise?
  • Which connected apps will Claude read from and write to, and do I approve them?
  • Who checks the formulas and numbers before the team uses them?

Prompts to try on day one

  • Build a campaign tracker with tabs for channel, owner and status, and a drop-down for status.
  • Make a team budget with a tab for each quarter and formulas for the totals.
  • Group these retro notes by theme and draft action items for each person.
