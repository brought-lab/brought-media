Setup steps, in order

  1. Confirm your Claude plan is Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise.
  2. Open the Claude for Microsoft 365 listing on Microsoft AppSource.
  3. Select Get it now to install.
  4. Open Excel and activate the add-in.
  5. Sign in with your Claude account.

Questions to ask before you start

  • Do your Claude usage limits cover this? Use is tied to your existing Claude account and its limits.
  • Does your company allow add-ins and sharing workbook data with Claude?
  • Is the spreadsheet from a source you trust?
  • Who reviews the changes before the file is shared?
  • Does the model use data tables? Those are on the official limitations list.

Prompts to try on day one

  • Explain how the total on the Summary tab is calculated. Cite the cells.
  • Find why this formula returns an error and suggest a fix.
  • Change the growth assumption to 4 percent and keep the formulas linked.
  • List every hardcoded number on this sheet.
  • Summarize what changed on this sheet and where.
