The Smart Reports starter kit

Anthropic

Setup steps, in order

  1. Ask a Primary Owner or Owner to turn on smart reports in organization settings.
  2. Check that your organization does not use CMEK, a HIPAA configuration or a hybrid deployment.
  3. Choose a team, a range of up to 28 days, the products and any custom questions, then open the report when it is ready.

Questions to ask before you start

  • Does our setup qualify, with no CMEK, HIPAA or hybrid deployment?
  • Has the team been told what the report reads and why?
  • Who decides which recommendations to act on?

Prompts to try on day one

  • Which workstreams took the most spend for this team over the last 28 days?
  • Where do sessions hit friction, such as a missing connector or a failed tool call?
  • Which repeated workflows are worth packaging as shared skills?
