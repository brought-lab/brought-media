Setup steps, in order

  1. Book a demo with Datarails sales, since there is no self-serve signup.
  2. Work with Datarails to scope the setup and build your source connections.
  3. Open Excel, go to Home > Add-ins, and search for Datarails.
  4. Sign in with your Datarails credentials, or through your identity provider if your company uses SSO.
  5. Click Connect to link a workbook to a Filebox, and the button becomes Submit once it is linked.

Questions to ask before you start

  • What does the plan cost, and which modules and users does it include?
  • Which of our ERP, CRM and HRIS systems can Datarails connect to?
  • Who on our team can see each data model, and how are permissions set?
  • Who reviews the AI drafts before they reach the board or investors?
  • Is our Excel on Microsoft 365, and which operating systems do we use?

Prompts to try on day one

  • Draft a variance summary of last month's actuals against budget.
  • Build a first draft of the monthly board report from our connected data.
  • List the accounts that moved most this quarter and show the source for each.
  • Show me the data behind this number so I can check it.
  • Draft a forecast update using our current Excel model.
