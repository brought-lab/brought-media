Intercom

Setup steps, in order

  1. Go to Fin AI Agent, then Train, then Content, and enable the content Fin can use.
  2. Add public articles, documents, snippets or public URLs.
  3. Under Train, then Guidance, write plain instructions for your brand voice and policies.
  4. Choose whether users, leads or visitors see Fin.
  5. Set escalation under Train, then Escalation, and test with teammates before going live.

Questions to ask before you start

  • Which helpdesk do we use, and does Fin work with it?
  • Are our help articles current enough for Fin to answer from?
  • Who writes the guidance and reviews Fin's answers?
  • Which cases always go to a person?
  • What does a resolution cost us, and who checks the bill?

Prompts to try on day one

  • Answer in a calm, plain tone. Keep replies short and use only our enabled help articles.
  • If a customer asks for a refund, hand the conversation to the support team.
  • If a customer sounds frustrated or asks the same question twice, hand off to a person.
  • If the answer is not in our content, say so and hand off to the team.
  • Draft a Procedure from our help center for a multi-step task. I will review it before it goes live.
