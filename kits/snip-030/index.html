DataSnipper

Setup steps, in order

  1. Ask your DataSnipper contact or admin for the installation link.
  2. Run the .msi installer and follow the wizard.
  3. Open Excel and check that the DATASNIPPER tab appears in the ribbon.
  4. Ask your admin to add you in the User Management System.
  5. Add your documents with Document Organizer.

Questions to ask before you start

  • Is your company a DataSnipper customer, and who is your contact?
  • Does your computer meet the Windows, Excel and 16 GB RAM requirements?
  • Does your data policy allow these documents to be loaded?
  • Who reviews and signs off the output?
  • Which plan does your team have, and which features come with it?

Prompts to try on day one

  • Link one figure in your workbook to its source document with a Snip.
  • Follow the link from the figure and check that it matches the source document.
  • Cross-reference one set of figures across two documents and note any gaps.
  • If your plan includes agents, run one prebuilt agent such as three-way AP matching on a small sample and review each result.
